# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve Ξ-105. This is a small, non-commercial fan project, so focused fixes are preferred over large redesigns.

## Before you start

Open an issue before making a major visual, format, or directory change. Bug reports, validation improvements, documentation corrections, and carefully reviewed animation fixes are welcome.

Do not submit:

- ripped or traced official artwork, screenshots, logos, model-kit scans, or game assets;
- material copied from another artist without explicit compatible permission;
- generated material that you do not have permission to contribute;
- advertising, paid integrations, or other commercial content; or
- secrets, personal data, or unrelated files.

## Contribution terms

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

1. you created it or have sufficient rights to submit it;
2. it does not knowingly violate another person's rights;
3. you understand this is an unofficial fan project; and
4. you license your contribution under the applicable non-commercial terms in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

The licenses cover only rights you are authorized to grant. Gundam-related third-party rights remain excluded.

## Pull-request checklist

- Keep the v2 atlas at 1536 × 2288 pixels with 192 × 208 cells.
- Preserve transparent RGBA output.
- Rebuild affected previews.
- Update the relevant QA or validation report.
- Avoid unrelated file changes.
- Explain the visual or behavioral reason for the change.

For sprite changes, include a still or GIF that makes the difference easy to review.
